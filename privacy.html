Cushion Kings Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Cushion Kings ("the game") is made by Matthew Smith ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

We don't collect your data

The game does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal or usage data. It has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your career progress, settings and car setup are saved only on your device. We can't see them. If you delete the game or clear its data, they're removed.

Payment

Cushion Kings is a paid app with no in-app purchases. You buy it once from the Apple App Store or Google Play, and that payment is handled entirely by the store under its own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details.

Feedback email

If you tap Feedback, your own email app opens with a message to mattalansmith3@gmail.com. The message includes the game version and basic device info (screen size, browser/OS version) so we can reproduce bugs. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. We use your email only to reply, and we delete it if you ask.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete local data by uninstalling the game. To ask about or delete anything you've emailed us, contact mattalansmith3@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version at this address and update the date above.
