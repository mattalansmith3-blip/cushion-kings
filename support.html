FAQ

How do I steer? Slide the stick with your left thumb and hold GAS with your right. Lift off the gas to let the car rotate into the corner.

Are there in-app purchases or ads? No. Cushion Kings is a one-time paid app. Career mode and all tracks are included.

How do I get the gold helmet? Win a career season championship. It unlocks in the Garage.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data erases it. There's no account or cloud save.

Does it need internet? No. The game plays offline.

Privacy: we don't collect any data. See the privacy policy.
